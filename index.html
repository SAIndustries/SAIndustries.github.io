LOB Market Making

Can a passive market maker make money on Binance spot? An order-book recorder and event-driven backtester that models latency and queue position, tested on BTCUSDT and DOGEUSDT with a strict tune / test split. Order-book imbalance predicts the next move (69.5% out-of-sample accuracy, AUC 0.76), yet quoting at the touch loses ~1.2 bp — and a PnL decomposition shows adverse selection is almost the whole loss. Imbalance filters and Avellaneda–Stoikov quoting didn't fix it out of sample.

Quant-Signal-Lab

A leakage-safe, walk-forward research framework testing next-day ETF return predictions (ridge, gradient boosting, MLP) with volatility-targeted, cost-aware backtests. Skill judged by out-of-sample R², rank IC and Sharpe with confidence intervals and multiple-testing-corrected p-values; validated by 21 unit tests and null / planted-signal controls. Honest result: no model beat a simple baseline after costs.
