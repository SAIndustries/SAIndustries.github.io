01 — About

Technical depth, client-facing instincts.

I'm an MSc Data Science student at King's College London with a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science. Before London, I spent 1.5 years as a Software Engineer at LTI Mindtree, building AI-powered features for international hospitality, logistics and airline clients — and pitching ideas directly to them. One of those pitches became a production platform for a global hotel group's loyalty redemption team.

My first job meant moving to Mumbai with no Hindi, no Marathi and a tech stack I'd never used. Within three months I'd picked up Hindi, Java, full-stack development and a set of AI tools. That's how I like to work: get dropped into something unfamiliar, learn fast, ship.

Today I'm focused on quantitative research — building leakage-safe, statistically honest frameworks for testing whether a trading signal is real.
