Quant-Signal-Lab

A leakage-safe, walk-forward research framework for testing whether next-day return predictions on ETFs hold up out of sample.

  • Ridge, gradient boosting and MLP models evaluated with strict walk-forward splits — no look-ahead.
  • Volatility-targeted, transaction-cost-aware backtests.
  • Skill measured with out-of-sample R², rank IC and Sharpe ratios with confidence intervals and multiple-testing-corrected p-values.
  • Validated with 21 unit tests plus null and planted-signal controls.
